## Tips

`!support` opens a short menu: pick an amount and a coin (Bitcoin or Litecoin) and you get a payment address and a QR code. No account is needed. You can also type an amount yourself, for example `!support 7`. The amount can be from $1 to $500.

The payment is checked automatically, and you get a direct message when it confirms. Tips go to hosting costs.

## What supporters get

- A thank-you role in the support server.
- An optional place on the supporters page. Your name is hidden until you choose to show it with `!support public on`, and you can hide it again with `!support public off`.
- `!support status` shows how much you have tipped in total.

## Ads on the website

The public pages of the website may show a small banner to visitors who are not signed in. Signed-in users never see it, and it is never shown on the dashboard, in verification or appeal pages, or inside Discord apps.
