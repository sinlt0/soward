## Vote

`!vote` shows where to vote and how long your reward lasts. You can vote every 12 hours.

## Reward

Each vote gives you global no-prefix access, which lets you run commands without typing the prefix in every server. The timer restarts from the moment you vote. You also get a direct message when the vote is counted.

## Streak

Vote at least once every 36 hours to keep your streak going. `!vote` shows your total votes and your current streak.
