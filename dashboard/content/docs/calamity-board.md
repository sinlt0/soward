## Calamity board

`!board` shows four bosses that are generated fresh every day at midnight UTC: two Wardens, a Lord and a Calamity. Each has a random name, an element, and one or two affixes. Everyone sees the same board.

Use `!board hunt <1-4>` to fight one. Each boss can be fought once a day per player, win or lose. You cannot hunt while injured. Slash users can use `/board`.

Rewards grow with rank: spirit stones, materials and herbs for your realm, qi, essences of the boss's element, Rift Shards and **Realm Keys** from Lords and Calamities, and a chance at a gear drop.

## Elements

Techniques have an element: metal, wood, water, fire, earth or thunder. Every boss lists the element it is **weak to**. A matching technique deals 35% more damage, and a technique of the boss's own kind deals 20% less.

## Affixes

| Affix | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Regenerating | Heals 4% of its health every turn |
| Vampiric | Drinks part of the damage it deals |
| Thorned | Reflects part of your damage |
| Frenzied | Hits harder below half health |
| Venomous | Every hit also burns you |
| Armored | Takes far less from plain attacks |
| Evasive | One blow in five misses |
| Warded | Takes less damage for the first three turns |

## Weekly contracts

Weekly contracts now live together with the daily bounties. Use `!world bounty` (or `/world bounty`) to see both boards: the daily bounties, and three weekly contracts assigned automatically every Monday. Finished ones are claimed when you open the list. See [Secret Realms](/docs/secret-realms) for the keys they pay out.

## Animated fights

Every technique has its own prebuilt animation, shown right on your fight message. The animations are made ahead of time, so fights stay smooth and never hit Discord rate limits.
