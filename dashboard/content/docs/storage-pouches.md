Cultivators now collect **herbs** and **materials** and keep them in **storage pouches**. Pills, gear and weapons are not stored in pouches, only herbs and materials.

## Herbs

Every realm has its own herbs, one for each battle pill. Higher realms find rarer herbs.

| Pill | Yellow | Mystic | Earth | Heaven |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vigor Pill | Ember Root | Flame Lotus | Sunfire Blossom | Phoenix Bloom |
| Iron Skin Pill | Stone Moss | Iron Bark Fungus | Jade Ginseng | Diamond Orchid |
| Windstep Pill | Wind Orchid | Cloud Fern | Storm Lily | Sky Lotus |
| Fortune Pill | Lucky Clover | Golden Chrysanthemum | Dragon Blood Fruit | Heavenly Peach |

Brewing a pill now needs **2 herbs** for your realm on top of the materials and spirit stones. Herbs can also be sold on the marketplace and at auction.

## Finding herbs and materials

- `!world gather herbs` searches for herbs. You get a couple of kinds from your realm, a small chance of a herb from the next realm, and sometimes Spirit Fiber.
- `!world gather hunt` hunts for your realm's material and sometimes a Beast Hide.
- You can gather 8 times a day, and you cannot gather while injured.
- Herbs also drop from won fights and from exploring.

Spirit Fiber and Beast Hide are new materials used to make pouches.

## Storage pouches

You have two pouches. The **Herb Pouch** holds herbs and the **Material Pouch** holds ore, cores, fibre, hides and rift shards. Each one counts items, so 40 herbs take 40 space. Without an upgrade each holds 40.

| Pouch | Space | Spirit stones | Fiber or hide | Rift Shards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cloth | 100 | 2,000 | 5 | 0 |
| Silk | 250 | 10,000 | 15 | 0 |
| Spirit Silk | 600 | 50,000 | 30 | 5 |
| Qiankun | 1,500 | 250,000 | 60 | 15 |
| Void | 4,000 | 1,000,000 | 100 | 40 |

The Herb Pouch uses Spirit Fiber and the Material Pouch uses Beast Hide.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `!pouch` | Show both pouches and what the next upgrade costs |
| `!pouch upgrade <herb\|material>` | Buy the next tier of a pouch |
| `!pouch sell <item> [amount\|all]` | Sell herbs or materials to the shop to free space |

## When a pouch is full

Nothing is lost. If a pouch is full, new herbs or materials that you earn are **sold automatically** at shop value and the spirit stones go to you. The result message tells you when that happens. Items you already hold, items returned from the marketplace and items from cancelled auctions are never sold and can take a pouch over its limit, but nothing new will fit until you free space.

Shop value per item: herbs 6, 30, 120 or 500 spirit stones by realm; Demon Core 10, Spirit Iron 50, Thunder Crystal 200, Chaos Shard 800, Rift Shard 400, Spirit Fiber and Beast Hide 15.
