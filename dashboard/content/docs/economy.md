## Your wallet

`!coins` shows your balance, and `!coins <user>` shows someone else's. Coins belong to you, not to a server, so they work everywhere the bot is.

## Bought and earned coins

Your wallet keeps two kinds of coins. **Bought** coins come from top-ups. **Earned** coins come from votes and tips. Spending uses your earned coins first, so your bought coins stay available for giving away.

## Earning coins

- **Vote** with `!vote`. Every bot list you vote on gives coins, and you can vote again every 12 hours.
- **Support** with `!support`. Any tip gives the same number of coins, whatever the amount.
- **Buy** coins with Bitcoin or Litecoin using `!coins topup` (or `!premium buy coins`). The rate is $1 for 1,000 coins, and the coins arrive when the payment confirms.

`!coins earn` lists the current amounts.

## Spending coins

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `!premium coins` | Shows premium plans priced in coins |
| `!premium coins <plan>` | Buys premium for the server (needs Manage Server) |
| `!family upgrade` | Buys more marriage or adoption slots |
| `!clan create` | Starts a clan |
| `!clan deposit` | Adds coins to your clan's bank |

## Giving and ranking

`!coins give <user> <amount>` sends bought coins to someone. Earned coins cannot be given away. `!coins top` shows the richest users.
