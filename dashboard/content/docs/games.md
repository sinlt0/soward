## Play with a friend or with the bot

- `!tictactoe [user]` (`!ttt`)
- `!connect4 [user]` (`!c4`)
- `!rps [user]`

Leave out the user to play against the bot. The person who starts the game moves first. In Rock Paper Scissors both picks stay hidden until both players have chosen.

## Play with the whole channel

- `!hangman`: anyone can guess a letter or the whole word with the Guess button. Six wrong guesses and you lose.
- `!trivia [easy|medium|hard]`: you get 20 seconds and one try. Questions come from the Open Trivia Database.
- `!typerace`: the sentence appears as an image. The first person to type it exactly wins and sees their speed in words per minute.

Only one hangman, trivia question or typing race runs per channel at a time.

## Records

`!games stats [user]` shows wins, losses and draws for every game. `!games top <game>` shows the best players of a game, for example `!games top trivia`.

Games do not use coins, so nothing is won or lost besides bragging rights.
