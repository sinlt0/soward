Secret realms are floor-by-floor dungeons. Two open every day, one for each of two elements, and sometimes a **hidden realm** appears with more floors, tougher guardians and bigger rewards. Use `!realm` (or `/realm`) to see today's realms.

## Entering
`!realm enter <1-3>` costs a **Realm Key** (two for a hidden realm). You can enter three realms a day and cannot enter while injured. Every realm has five floors (seven for a hidden one) and a guardian at the end.

Realm Keys drop from Lords and Calamities on the [calamity board](/docs/calamity-board), from the daily bounty chest and from the weekly contract chest. Clearing a realm sometimes returns a key.

## Floors
Your health carries from floor to floor, so you have to manage it. A floor can be:

| Floor | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Monster or elite | A fight against a creature of the realm's element. |
| Treasure | Break the ward for double loot with a risk of being burned, or unseal it carefully for safe loot. |
| Spring | Recover 40% health. |
| Shrine | Pray for might (+15% damage for the rest of the run, costs some health) or for vigor (heal 25%). |

You can retreat with your loot between floors. If you fall you keep half of what you gathered, and you lose a little qi. Clearing the guardian pays extra and has a chance at gear.

## Essences and attunement
Realms and calamity bosses drop **essences** of an element. `!realm attune <element>` spends 3 essences to attune you for 12 hours: techniques of that element deal **25% more damage**, stacking with elemental weaknesses. Each realm and boss lists the element it is weak to, so pick the essence that matches.

## How it connects
- Calamity bosses and bounty chests drop Realm Keys.
- Realms drop essences, materials, herbs and gear. Essences make your next boss hunts stronger.
- Entering and clearing realms count toward daily bounties and weekly contracts.
- New titles: Realm Breaker, Realm Sovereign, Six Elements Walker and Calamity Slayer.
