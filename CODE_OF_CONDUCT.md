# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Soward is a small open-source project maintained by Sinlt and the Soward
Team. Everyone participating — through issues, pull requests,
discussions, or any other project space — is expected to help keep it a
welcoming, harassment-free environment.

**Examples of expected behavior:**

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users.

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Plagiarizing the project — presenting this codebase, or a trivial
  rebrand of it, as your own original work (see `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`).
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue
or contacting Sinlt directly. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated, and the maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues,
or pull requests, or ban contributors, for behavior they deem
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
